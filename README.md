## 💡 Selected Work

### 🧠 Foxwrite.ai — AI Campaign Reporting Platform

An AI-powered reporting system that transforms raw **Meta Ads and Google Ads exports** into polished, client-ready reports.

- Reduced a **1–3 hour manual reporting workflow to under a minute**
- Built a **trust-first architecture** where advertising metrics are calculated deterministically
- Uses LLMs for narrative generation only after the underlying numbers are verified
- Includes analytics, wasted-spend detection and report generation

**Tech:** `Python` `FastAPI` `Pandas` `Anthropic` `React` `Generative AI`

---

### 🧬 Medical RAG Assistant

Built a domain-specific **Retrieval-Augmented Generation system** for biomedical research and medical information.

- Processed and indexed thousands of biomedical research documents
- Built semantic retrieval using biomedical embeddings and vector search
- Generated grounded answers with source citations
- Developed patient-friendly and technical response modes

**Tech:** `Python` `FastAPI` `RAG` `Qdrant` `PubMedBERT` `LLMs` `React`
